About IDM Consulting & Associates

IDM Consulting & Associates is a registered research, monitoring, evaluation, and management consulting firm (Reg. July 2003, Business Names Registration Act CAP. 46:02). We deliver high-quality technical advisory services to government institutions, development partners, bilateral and multilateral organizations, non-governmental organizations, and the private sector.

Our mission is to promote and strengthen the sustainable development agenda by providing actionable insights and robust institutional management support.
